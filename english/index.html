Zhixin Li 李智鑫

I am an Associate Researcher (Young Scholar) at the Institute of Financial Technology, Fudan University. I currently serve as Deputy Director of the Shanghai Basic Research Innovation Center for Cross-Border Data Applications and Deputy Director of the Office of the Institute of Financial Technology, Fudan University.

My research focuses on AI-enabled financial risk management, with an emphasis on anomaly detection in complex transaction networks, privacy protection and trustworthy collaboration across institutions, and the application of large language models and multi-agent systems in financial settings. My work spans temporal graph anomaly detection, multi-agent systems, trustworthy federated learning, and LLM inference optimization and security.

Research interests
FinTech; Temporal Graph Anomaly Detection; Multi-Agent Systems; Inference Optimization for Financial Large Language Models; Financial Risk Management; AI4FinTech.

Research

  • Temporal graph learning and financial risk identification

    Graph anomaly detection, dynamic risk identification, anti-money laundering benchmarks, and synthetic transaction data for complex financial networks.

  • Privacy-preserving and trustworthy collaboration

    Federated learning, privacy protection, and collaborative modeling across institutions under constraints on data sharing.

  • Multi-agent systems and financial risk simulation

    Agent interactions, strategic behavior, and risk propagation in financial settings, with an emphasis on controlled experimentation and simulation.

  • Financial LLMs and inference optimization

    Efficient inference, security, and trustworthy use of large language models in finance, including task-specific adaptation and optimization.
